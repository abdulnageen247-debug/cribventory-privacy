CribVentory Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

CribVentory ("the app") is published by Mutch ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone. We do not run any servers for the app, we have no user accounts, and we cannot see or access what you store.

Information stored on your device

The app lets you record items in your home, such as appliances and furniture, along with dimensions, purchase details, warranty and service dates, notes, photos and documents. All of this is stored locally on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app.

If you use Settings to save a backup, the backup file is created on your device and you choose where it goes using the Android share sheet. We do not receive it.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission so you can scan barcodes and take photos of items and documents. Images are processed and stored on your device only.

Notifications

The app can schedule local reminders for warranties and servicing. These are created on your device and do not use any push notification server.

Barcode lookups (internet)

When you scan a barcode, the app first checks its built-in catalogue and any barcodes you have taught it. If nothing is found, it may send only the barcode number to the public product databases UPCitemdb (upcitemdb.com) and Open Products Facts (openproductsfacts.org) to look up the product name. No account details, photos, names or other data from your home are sent. Those services receive your device's IP address as part of any normal internet request and have their own privacy policies.

Links to retailers

The app can show links to retailers such as Amazon, Currys, AO, Argos, John Lewis, eBay and others, including spare-parts shops. Opening a link takes you to that retailer's website in your browser, which is governed by their privacy policy. Some links may be affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission if you buy, at no extra cost to you. The app itself does not share any of your stored data with these retailers.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or have access to your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics tools in the app.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not require you to create an account.

Your control

Because data lives on your device, you control it. You can edit or delete any item inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings permanently deletes all of its data. Make a backup first if you want to keep it. You can withdraw camera permission at any time in Android settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date above.
